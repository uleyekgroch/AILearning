Weather changes every day. Scientists study weather with instruments.
They measure temperature, wind speed, and air pressure.
Clouds are made of tiny water drops. Rain falls when clouds get heavy.
The water cycle moves water from the ocean to the sky and back again.
Plants need sunlight and water to grow. Their roots take water from the soil.
Leaves use sunlight to make food. This process is called photosynthesis.
Animals adapt to their environment. Polar bears have thick fur for cold weather.
Desert animals can survive with very little water. Camels store fat in their humps.
The Earth rotates once every twenty four hours. This gives us day and night.
The moon travels around the Earth. It takes about twenty eight days.
Simple machines help us do work. Levers, pulleys, and wheels are simple machines.
A lever is a stiff bar that turns on a point called a fulcrum.
