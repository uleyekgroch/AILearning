Climate change represents one of the most significant challenges facing humanity.
Rising global temperatures affect ecosystems, weather patterns, and sea levels.
Greenhouse gases trap heat in the atmosphere, causing the planet to warm gradually.
Carbon dioxide from burning fossil fuels is the primary driver of this warming.
Scientists use complex computer models to predict future climate conditions.
These models incorporate data from satellites, weather stations, and ocean buoys.
The Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change provides assessments based on thousands of studies.
Mitigation strategies include renewable energy, carbon capture, and reforestation.
Adaptation involves building resilient infrastructure and developing drought-resistant crops.
International cooperation through agreements like the Paris Accord aims to limit global warming.
Biodiversity loss accelerates as habitats shrink and species struggle to adapt.
Conservation efforts protect endangered species and preserve critical ecosystems.
The development of artificial intelligence raises important ethical and societal questions.
Machine learning algorithms can identify patterns in vast datasets beyond human capability.
Natural language processing enables computers to understand and generate human language.
Computer vision systems can recognize objects, faces, and scenes from digital images.
